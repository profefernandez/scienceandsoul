Our Philosophy
Clinical rigor and spiritual practice, working together

Clinical Foundation: Kelly draws on CBT, DBT, trauma-focused care, and psychodynamic therapy, selecting methods tailored to each client's specific goals.

Spiritual Practices: Sessions can include chakra alignment, Reiki, and sound bowl healing, addressing emotional and energetic aspects of wellbeing.

Client Strengths: Kelly builds on the insight and resilience clients already possess, developing skills that carry forward after sessions.

Services Offered
Individual Therapy, Group Therapy, & Integrative Care
Kelly offers personalized therapy sessions, integrating spiritual modalities. Each session is customized to your unique goals and needs.

The Process
A Structured Process, Tailored to Each Person

Start with the Full Picture: Understanding your history and goals.

Apply Clinical Methods: Adapting CBT, DBT, and mindfulness based on what works for you.

Add Spiritual Practices When Useful: Integrating Reiki, chakra alignment, and sound bowl healing as needed.

Build Skills That Last: Developing coping strategies and self-understanding that empower you beyond therapy.

Who We Serve
Teens, Adults, and the LGBTQIA+ Community

Teens & Adults: Support for trauma, anxiety, ADHD/ADD, grief, and more.

LGBTQIA+ Community: Affirming care addressing identity, family dynamics, and unique stressors.

Clients Seeking Integrative Care: Combining clinical therapy with spiritual practices to meet diverse needs.
